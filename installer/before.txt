MD Vertex Measure v1.0.0
Author: MD (Mayank Dholariya)

A panel + pick tool for Autodesk Navisworks Manage / Simulate 2017 to 2027 (64-bit).
Shows E3D-like snap bubbles (center, ends, end quadrants, corners, face centers)
on selected piping, structural steel and equipment so you can pick two bubbles
and read the distance. Ships alongside other MD Navis tools.

Needs Navisworks Manage or Simulate. Navisworks Freedom cannot load plugins.

Please close Navisworks before continuing.
